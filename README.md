# DasherZ PocketSync Privacy Policy

**Developer:** DasherZ  
**Effective date:** 31 October 2026  
**Contact:** dasherz.pocketsync@gmail.com

DasherZ PocketSync (PocketSync) lets people play their own audio together and share fullscreen designs across Android phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. This policy explains how the app and its optional account and purchase services handle information.

## 1. Music and local storage

You choose audio through Android’s file picker. PocketSync reads the selected files, their names and audio metadata. It may save source files or decoded audio in app storage for caching, preloading and replay.

Playlists, recent songs, preferences and the most recent room are stored locally.

When you play or share audio in a room, that audio and related playback information are transferred to participating phones. Those phones may retain cached copies. Clearing your own phone does not erase copies already received by another participant.

PocketSync does not upload your songs, playlists or room history to a developer-operated cloud music library. It does not scan your entire music library or require access to contacts, photos, microphone recordings or physical location.

## 2. Room and device information

Local discovery and synchronization exchange information needed to connect participating phones, including:

- Room names and phone labels.
- Local network addresses.
- App-generated device and peer identifiers.
- Screen dimensions and arrangement.
- Timing, connection and readiness information.
- Playback and visual-control state.

Room information is visible to relevant phones on the local network or in the room. A host approves joining phones. Local discovery uses Wi-Fi networking, not GPS or Bluetooth.

## 3. Optional Google sign-in

Basic listening does not require a PocketSync account.

If you choose Google sign-in, subscription or purchase restoration, Google supplies an identity token that can contain your account identifier, email address and basic profile information.

Our service verifies the token and derives an account identifier used to associate sessions and purchases. The phone stores an encrypted account session and an installation key used to authenticate requests.

We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Google Drive or your contacts.

## 4. Purchases and Premium

Google Play handles subscription payments.

PocketSync receives purchase tokens, plan identifiers, subscription status, expiry and acknowledgement information to verify and restore Premium. An account identifier is passed to Google Play to associate the purchase with the correct PocketSync account.

Purchase tokens are encrypted in the private billing database. We do not receive or store your card number, UPI credentials, bank login or complete payment details. Google handles its own account and transaction information under its privacy notices.

The current unlocked Premium preview does not require payment. Any paid offer must display its actual price and billing period in Google Play before purchase.

## 5. Service information and support

Our hosting services process network requests, which can include IP address, request time, endpoint, response status and technical request information.

Authentication challenges, sessions, replay-protection values and rate limits help protect account and purchase services.

If you contact support, we receive the information you send. Please do not send passwords, Google sign-in tokens, raw purchase tokens, private keys or complete payment details.

The app does not include an advertising SDK or a developer analytics/crash-reporting service. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising profiles. Google Play and Android may provide their own platform diagnostics under their notices.

## 6. Service providers and disclosures

Google provides sign-in, Play billing and subscription verification. Our billing service runs on Google Cloud and stores private billing records on Supabase.

These providers process information needed for authentication, purchases, storage, security and operation. Information may be processed outside your country.

Room information and selected audio are transferred to participating phones. We do not send selected audio to Google Cloud or Supabase for music hosting.

GitHub hosts these policy pages and processes website requests under its own hosting and privacy practices. We have not added analytics scripts or tracking cookies to these pages.

We may disclose limited information when required by law or necessary to protect users, the service or legal rights.

## 7. Security

Online account and billing requests use HTTPS. Database connections verify TLS certificates. Account sessions are encrypted on the phone, and stored purchase tokens are encrypted.

Local room discovery and audio/control transfers are not end-to-end encrypted by PocketSync. They use ordinary TCP/UDP on your Wi-Fi or hotspot. A room code or host approval does not encrypt the audio stream.

Use networks and participants you trust. No software or network can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data retention

Local data remains until you remove it, clear app storage, uninstall, or the app or Android evicts cached data. Clearing PocketSync data does not delete original audio files from your own storage.

Account and purchase records are retained while needed to provide or restore account access and subscriptions, unless removed through a verified deletion request or retained for a necessary legal or security reason.

Cancelling a subscription or uninstalling the app does not automatically erase these records.

Technical logs and any recovery backups follow the hosting providers’ configured retention and expiry. Support correspondence is retained while handling a request and any necessary follow-up, dispute or legal obligation, then removed when no longer needed.

Any limited retention after deletion is restricted to its necessary purpose.

## 9. Account and data deletion

To request access, correction or deletion of your PocketSync account information, email:

**dasherz.pocketsync@gmail.com**

For account deletion, use the subject:

**PocketSync account deletion**

You can submit a request without reinstalling the app. If an account reference is already available, you may include it.

We may verify ownership before acting and will explain any additional steps needed to identify the account. Do not send passwords, Google sign-in tokens, raw purchase tokens, private keys or complete payment details.

Verified requests are handled without undue delay under applicable requirements. Associated account data is removed from active systems unless limited legal or security retention applies. Necessary logs and recovery backups follow the retention criteria above.

The current app does not yet provide an in-app account-deletion control; email is the available request route.

Deletion does not remove:

- Your Google account.
- Google’s transaction records.
- Original audio files in your own storage.
- Copies already received by another room participant.

Clear PocketSync’s app storage through Android Settings to remove its local data from your phone.

Deleting the app or PocketSync account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage renewal through Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

An account-deletion request is not an automatic cancellation or refund request.

## 10. Younger users

PocketSync is not directed to children under 13.

Minors should obtain any parental permission required for account or purchase services under applicable local rules.

If you believe a child supplied personal information through our account service, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 11. Your choices

You can:

- Use basic listening without Google sign-in.
- Choose which audio files to share.
- Leave a listening room.
- Manage local playlists and preferences.
- Clear PocketSync’s local app storage.
- Request account information or deletion by email.
- Manage subscription renewal through Google Play.

Your privacy rights may vary by location, including rights to request access, correction or deletion and to raise a concern with a local data-protection authority.
